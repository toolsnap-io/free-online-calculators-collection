## Why Free Online Calculators?

Free calculators save time and reduce errors compared to manual calculations or spreadsheet setups. The best tools offer:

- **Instant results** — no signup or installation required
- **Privacy** — calculations happen in your browser, data never leaves your device
- **Mobile-friendly** — works on any device without downloading apps
